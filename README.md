# 🌍 Wander – Interactive World Explorer

**Explore the world. Discover cultures. Find your next destination.**

Wander is an interactive world exploration website built to help users discover countries, explore different cultures, compare destinations, and plan their travel experiences through a user-friendly interface.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://wander-world-around.netlify.app/

## ✨ Features

### 🗺️ Interactive World Exploration

* Explore different regions and countries through an interactive interface.
* Discover destinations around the world.
* Access country-specific information.

### ❤️ Wishlist

* Save favourite destinations.
* Keep track of places you want to explore.

### ⚖️ Country Comparison

* Compare countries and discover their differences.
* Explore destinations to help make travel decisions.

### 🎭 Culture Shock

* Discover cultural differences, traditions, and interesting facts about countries around the world.

### 🧭 Match Me

* Answer a series of questions to discover destinations that match your interests and preferences.

### ✈️ Plan Trip

* Explore potential travel destinations.
* Discover countries that interest you and organise your travel ideas.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* **HTML5** – Website structure.
* **CSS3** – Styling, layouts, animations, and responsive design.
* **JavaScript** – Interactive elements and dynamic functionality.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Make world exploration engaging and interactive.
* Encourage users to discover countries and cultures.
* Help users explore and compare travel destinations.
* Provide an engaging platform for discovering places that match individual preferences.
